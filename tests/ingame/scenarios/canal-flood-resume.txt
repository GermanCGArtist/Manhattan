// canal 4r (run_tests.py canal_flood_resume): the live run's 9-hour loop. The supply chest cannot be opened (a block
// on top) and the want-list asks for more buckets than there are: restock keeps failing. flood() must carry on after
// each interruption (same cells, same skips) and finish, and restock_needed must not fire every minute.
stall(2)
option("survival.supply", pos(96, -60, 96))
want("bucket", 40)
enable("restock")
on restock_needed do restock() end
select(pos(100, -62, 100), pos(108, -61, 110))
print("TEST flood start")
flood()
